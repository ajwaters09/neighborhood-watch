How this is measured

  1. Monthly counts for each area, on a log scale so big and small areas move comparably.
  2. Each area's own average is removed (big areas have more of everything), and so is each month's citywide average (summer raises everything everywhere).
  3. What's left, "unusually high for this area this month", is correlated between 311 in month t and crime in month t+1, +2, +3.
  4. A pair is flagged only if it's statistically significant after correcting for every pair tested, stronger than the same-month link, and stronger than the reverse direction (crime coming before 311).

Correlation, not causation. A flagged pair is a citywide historical pattern worth watching. It isn't proof that one causes the other, and it isn't a forecast for any single area. Computed by notebooks/05_leading_indicators.py.
